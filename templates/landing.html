FAQ

Questions before starting a matter.

What does Case Closed do?+

It helps litigators organize matter documents, develop facts and timelines, research relevant case law, and prepare editable first drafts in one connected workspace.

Is Case Closed a replacement for legal research?+

No. It assists with research by using matter context to find and explain potentially relevant authority. Lawyers should inspect every source independently.

Can I inspect the cited authority?+

Yes. Case results include citations, jurisdiction information, relevance explanations, and links for reviewing the source.

What types of documents can I upload?+

The current workspace supports PDF, DOCX, DOC, and TXT matter documents.

Can I edit and export drafts?+

Yes. Generated memo and brief drafts remain editable and can be exported for continued lawyer review.

Does Case Closed replace lawyer review?+

No. Case Closed provides assistance with organization, research, and drafting. Its output must be reviewed by a qualified attorney before use.
